01.

Soft Scalpture

Sewing

Tufting

Soft sculpture made up of a sewn humanoid fabric doll resting on a tufted rug base. This sculpture is designed to be both visually and tactically pleasing.

Through the colour, texture and form, this sculpture explores the relationship between humans and the land we exist on. Many indigenous communities often view themselves as belonging to the land they live on, as opposed to land as a commodity that one can own.

02.

Accessible Tactile Representations

Sewing

3D Printing

Accessibility

Experimenting with creating accessible tactile representations of visual artworks exhibited in the Poster House Museum. Most tactile graphics only focus on creating clear visual forms, but these representations use fabric to create a more present tactile experience.

Adobe Illustrator was used to simplify and organise the shapes. Blender and 3D printing on mesh were used to create more intricate shapes like text.
